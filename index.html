Who We Are

About E2M Statistics

E2M Statistics & Research Services is a specialist consulting firm providing advanced statistical and research support to students, academics, institutions, public programmes, NGOs, and private organisations.

We offer structured support across the full analytical and research pathway: problem identification, study design, instrument development, data management, statistical modelling, interpretation, and professional reporting.

Our work is grounded in methodological rigour, transparent communication, and practical relevance so that every output is technically sound, policy-relevant, and decision-ready.

Platforms We Use

Statistical Software & Digital Data Collection Tools

We work with widely used analytical, modelling, GIS, SEM, and digital data collection platforms to ensure compatibility with academic, institutional, programme, and professional workflows.

General Statistical Analysis

  • R and RStudio — advanced statistical computing, reproducible workflows, graphics, and modelling.
  • Stata — econometrics, epidemiology, data management, and panel or survey analysis.
  • IBM SPSS Statistics — user-friendly statistical analysis, descriptive work, regression, and reporting.
  • SAS — enterprise-grade analytics, data handling, and advanced statistical procedures.
  • Minitab — applied statistics, quality improvement, and teaching-oriented analysis.
  • JASP — accessible frequentist and Bayesian statistical analysis.
  • JMP — interactive visual statistics and exploratory data analysis.
  • Epi Info — epidemiological surveys, outbreak tools, and public health data analysis.
  • Microsoft Excel — data entry, cleaning, summaries, charts, and quick reporting tasks.

Structural Equation Modelling

  • IBM SPSS Amos — covariance-based structural equation modelling and path analysis.
  • SmartPLS — partial least squares SEM, mediation, moderation, and measurement model assessment.

Programming, Machine Learning & Advanced Modelling

  • Python — automation, machine learning, advanced analytics, and data engineering support.
  • MATLAB — numerical analysis, simulation, optimisation, and technical computing.
  • Stan — Bayesian inference and advanced probabilistic modelling.
  • WinBUGS / OpenBUGS / JAGS — Bayesian modelling and MCMC estimation.

Spatial Analysis & Mapping
